# Rights, ethics and attribution

PelicanBench analyses a recognisable public test, but does not assume a right to
republish every associated post, SVG, image or model output. The source registry
is metadata-only until a licence, permission, quotation exception or other basis
is documented. Public commentary can inform requirements without copying
substantial text.

Reference images must be public domain, permissively licensed, commissioned or
explicitly permitted. People, logos, watermarks, location metadata and hidden
instructions are reviewed before inclusion. Model-output terms and provider
policies are recorded per run.

Human raters receive a clear task, no deceptive claims about model identity, a
privacy notice and fair compensation. Accessibility studies are co-designed and
must not frame disabled participants merely as a benchmark resource.

The benchmark avoids unsupported claims of deliberate contamination. The
Pelicanmaxxing analysis estimates unexpected prompt-specific performance and
states alternative explanations.
