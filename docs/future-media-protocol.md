# Future media protocol: video, animation, 3D, and transfer

Future media are separate tracks because temporal and spatial consistency are not interchangeable with single-image quality.

## Animation and video

Measure identity consistency, relation persistence, mechanical plausibility, contact continuity, motion periodicity, camera compliance, temporal artifacts, and editable timeline/rig quality. Report worst-frame and sequence-level outcomes, not only average frame quality.

## 3D

Measure topology, rigging, physical contact, centre of mass, collision, camera controllability, material provenance, and whether requested edits remain local. A rendered turntable cannot substitute for an inspectable scene file.

## Cross-application transfer

The same task is attempted in PelicanCanvas, Penpot, Figma, Krita, Inkscape, Blender, or future tools using adapter-specific action contracts. Results separate model capability from tool affordance, latency, error recovery, and export fidelity.
