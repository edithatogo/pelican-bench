# V1 pilot amendment 001: empirical bridge and balanced confirmatory panel

## Problem identified

The original 16-scenario alpha design paired particular animals with particular mobile objects inside several interface strata. It was useful for software integration but could not cleanly identify separate animal, object and interaction effects.

## Decision

The original files remain immutable historical alpha artifacts. The prospective candidate now has three explicitly separated panels:

1. The exact heritage prompt, reported independently.
2. A source-locked 8 by 6 Castillo bridge for replication and external comparability.
3. A primary confirmatory panel crossing four body plans with eight objects across four interface strata, using two prompt formulations.

The source-locked bridge retains `plane` because changing it would defeat exact replication; its known geometric ambiguity is recorded. The confirmatory panel avoids that ambiguity and uses affordance-specific relations.

## Consequences

The candidate has 113 tasks. Staged allocation produces 18 heritage trials, 1,008 replication trials and 1,152 confirmatory trials, for 2,178 prospective generations before judge and human-calibration calls. The union cohort first completes 63 qualification cells.

The principal inference is based on the balanced interface panel. Heritage and bridge results are secondary and cannot dominate the primary composite. The exact replication panel is analysed both under its original scoring logic and PelicanBench's measurement-validity framework.
