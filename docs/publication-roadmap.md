# Publication roadmap

## Substack series

1. **Why the pelican still matters:** the joke, Simon's longitudinal test and the
   difference between a memorable probe and a secure benchmark.
2. **When a blob beats the metric:** structural rewards, shortcut learning and
   why scorecards need semantic and human calibration.
3. **The third ontology:** animal, vehicle and the relation that makes “riding”
   true, including why a tuk-tuk changes the problem.
4. **The contact sheet is the result:** agentic drawing, trajectories, tools,
   persistence and critique.
5. **Pelicanmaxxing without conspiracy:** a transparent interaction analysis.
6. **Launching PelicanBench V1:** methods, limitations, open data and invitation
   to reproduce or break the scorer.

## Scholarly outputs

- Methods/preprint: dynamic compositional visual evaluation with ontology-driven
  tasks, scorecards, drift and human calibration.
- Dataset/benchmark card and citable software release.
- Agentic drawing paper focused on trajectory evaluation and skill acquisition.
- Longitudinal analysis of the historical pelican corpus after rights clearance.

The arXiv manuscript is future state; the repository begins with a skeleton,
registered hypotheses, tables/figure plan and reproducible artifact manifest.
