# Research questions and hypotheses

1. Does performance on the exact pelican–bicycle prompt exceed that predicted by
   general animal and mobile-object performance?
2. Which failures are anatomical, mechanical, relational, compositional,
   structural or instruction-following failures?
3. How rapidly do automatic scorers drift away from human judgements as systems
   improve?
4. Does a shared real-image reference pack reduce species/mechanics errors, and
   does it change style diversity?
5. Do source-level SVG metrics add predictive validity beyond raster-only judges?
6. Can repair tasks distinguish visual diagnosis from one-shot generation skill?
7. Does agentic tool use improve final quality enough to justify its latency,
   cost and regression risk?
8. Do explicit critique and contact-sheet review improve trajectory efficiency?
9. Can a learned skill transfer from pelican+bicycle to held-out animal–vehicle
   interfaces without exact-task overfitting?
10. Are language-driven drawing agents materially more usable for participants
    who face barriers in conventional design software?
