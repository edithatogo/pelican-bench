# Accessibility and language-driven creation protocol

PelicanBench’s accessibility track asks whether language-mediated drawing gives people meaningful control over an editable artifact, not merely whether a model can produce an attractive image.

## Co-design principles

Participants with motor, visual, cognitive, or interface-access barriers should shape tasks, interaction design, stopping rules, and outcomes. The protocol avoids assuming that one disability group has homogeneous goals.

## Outcomes

Task completion, correction success, edit locality, time, action burden, assistance required, confidence, perceived control, frustration, and artifact ownership are measured. Qualitative accounts are analysed alongside performance. Conventional mouse/keyboard interaction is not treated as the only reference condition.

## Safeguards

Alternative text, keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, scalable views, reduced motion, and non-colour-only cues are required. Human support can be requested without automatically invalidating a run; support type is recorded. Participant data is minimised and separated from benchmark artifacts.

## Maturity gate

No accessibility claim may be made from simulation alone. The track remains exploratory until co-designed participant work is ethically approved, conducted, and independently reviewed.
