A future human-classification workflow

No responses are collected. This local preview is not a recruitment invitation, consent instrument or active study. Do not enter or send personal information. All example answer controls are disabled.

The owner is the approver, not a required rater. Agents prepare and test the software; their advice is synthetic and non-independent. Later contributors could supply human classifications only through a separately approved service.

Before anyone could take part

  1. The approver reviews a named protocol, operator, custodian and contact, eligibility, exact instructions, workload and supported accommodations.
  2. A separately approved service presents its versioned consent and privacy notice before any study image. This preview cannot record consent.
  3. Eligible volunteers receive approved practice and only their authorized assignments. This page contains no study assignments.
  4. A private durable service confirms a save only after committing it. No save, retry or resume operation exists in this preview.

Approval to publish instructions is separate from approval to collect classifications. If private storage or participant safeguards are not ready, the workflow stays demonstration-only.

Private responses, voluntary participation

This page has no account, submission, storage, analytics or withdrawal operation. Navigating these sections changes no session state. Closing the page leaves no classification record in this application.

A future service must explain how to skip, stop and request withdrawal, including its approved deadline and deletion limits. No retention period, anonymity guarantee or erasure promise is approved here. Do not send responses through public issues, discussions or dataset repositories.

If a future save fails, the service must not claim success. Actual isolation, recovery, withdrawal and backup handling require tests before activation.

Human contributions can be considered later

Public workflow code does not mean public raw responses. Future contributions stay private and bound to approved protocol, consent and asset versions. Contributions remain quarantined until eligibility and integrity checks pass.

Analysis, restricted joins, promotion and publication of results each need separate approval. Submitting a classification would not automatically change the benchmark. Any approved incorporation creates a new derived version; historical frozen evidence is preserved.

Volunteer participation and owner approval do not establish independence or representativeness. Agent advice is never relabeled as human classification.
