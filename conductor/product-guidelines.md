# Product guidelines

## Voice

Curious, precise, lightly playful and non-hyped. Explain failures as information. Avoid anthropomorphism when a mechanism or observation is available.

## Visual identity

The pelican and bicycle are the recognisable anchor. Scientific figures should remain legible in monochrome, carry direct labels and expose uncertainty. Public pages may be whimsical; benchmark cards, manifests and results must remain restrained and accessible.

## UX principles

- Show why a score was assigned and which evidence supports it.
- Preserve original output, canonical render and analysis overlay.
- Separate public anchors from sealed challenge results.
- Distinguish observed, inferred, human-rated and model-judged fields.
- Make failure states and missing rights explicit.
- Support keyboard-only and text-first use.
