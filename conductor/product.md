# Product definition

## Vision

Make visual-generation progress measurable as a reproducible scientific trajectory rather than a sequence of attractive anecdotes, while preserving the humour and accessibility of a pelican attempting to ride a bicycle.

## Mission

PelicanBench builds open, inspectable and longitudinal evaluation infrastructure for compositional visual generation, editable vector artifacts, image repair and agentic drawing. It combines dynamic tasks, explicit ontologies, source-level analysis, human-calibrated judgement and full run provenance.

## Purpose

The project exists to answer three linked questions:

1. Can a system construct the requested entities and their physical relationship rather than optimise superficial proxies?
2. Can it inspect, revise and improve an editable artifact through tools and feedback?
3. Can the resulting claims remain comparable, auditable and resistant to benchmark contamination over time?

## Users

Model developers, evaluation researchers, agent/tool builders, visual artists, accessibility researchers, educators and readers interested in the evolution of AI capability.

## Product principles

- Fun at the surface, rigorous underneath.
- Vectors of evidence before a single opaque reward.
- Dynamic and sealed tasks alongside a permanent public anchor.
- Artifacts and trajectories, not screenshots alone.
- Human calibration and uncertainty, not judge-model authority.
- Rights-aware historical preservation.
- Open contracts with implementation independence.
